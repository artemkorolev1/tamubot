## Course Description, Outcomes, and Prerequisites

This course provides an introductory, hands-on treatment of broad aspects of the emerging field of data analytics for engineers, with focus on statistical learning and predictive modeling methods. The course is designed mainly for master students pursuing professional degrees. At the end of this course, students should be able to

- (1) Learn the basic techniques of statistical learning and predictive modeling;

- (2) Learn  the  principles  of  building  and  validating  modern  statistical  models  for  engineering applications;

- (3) Demonstrate your knowledge by implementing existing techniques, developing new techniques to solve a real-world problem, and conducting a comprehensive and insightful review study on any emerging area of statistical learning.

Prerequisites: Basic knowledge in probability, statistics and linear algebra is required. Prior programming experience is a plus.
