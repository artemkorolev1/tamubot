## Additional Course Details


This  senior/graduate-level  course  introduces  optical,  laser,  and  imaging-based sensing and measurement methods for advanced manufacturing processes. Emphasis is placed on in-situ and real-time process monitoring, measurement, and characterization  techniques  used  in  Additive  Manufacturing  and  semiconductor manufacturing.  Students  will  learn  the  physical  principles,  system  integration,  and practical limitations of advanced sensing modalities, and how measurement data can be used to understand, characterize, and improve manufacturing process performance.


## Course Prerequisites


Prerequisite/Corequisite(s): Approval of instructor.


## Course Learning Outcomes


Upon completion of this course, the learner will be able to:

- Explain the physical principles underlying optical, laser, and imaging-based sensing and measurement techniques used in advanced manufacturing processes.

- Design in-situ sensing and monitoring strategies to characterize key process variables such as temperature, geometry, material state, and process stability.

- Analyze and interpret sensor data to extract meaningful process information, including thermal behavior, material response, and process dynamics.

- Assess measurement uncertainty, limitations, and sources of error in optical and laser-based sensing systems and propose mitigation strategies.

- Apply sensing and measurement concepts to real-world case studies in advanced manufacturing processes
