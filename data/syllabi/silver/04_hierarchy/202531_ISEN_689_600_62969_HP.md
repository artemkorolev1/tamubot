# Course Description

This course covers the basic theory of sets, functions, numbers, linear algebra, and real analysis necessary for graduate coursework in operations research. Engineering PhD students focusing in intensely mathematical or theoretical fields will also find this material to be integral to their work. Material covered includes:

- Foundations: Sets, functions, numbers, relations, orders, products, and fields

- Linear Algebra: Vector spaces, linear maps, isomorphisms, dual spaces, spectral theorems for operators on finite-dimensional real vector spaces, multilinear algebra

- Real Analysis: Sequences in general metric spaces, series in ℝ , topology of metric spaces with emphasis on finite-dimensional real normed and inner product spaces, continuity, differentiation, and integration

We will omit many topics from standard courses in real analysis and linear algebra that may not be of interest to engineering PhDs such as linear algebra over complex and finite fields, and the rigorous construction of various common functions in mathematics.

# Course Expectations

It is expected by the end of the course that you will be able to quickly draft proofs of basic claims involving mathematical foundations, linear algebra, and real analysis.

### Planned Topics

The course is organized into the following modules:

### Foundations :

1. Basic Set & Function Theory
2. Relations and Orders
3. Basic Number Theory, Ordered Fields, and Polynomials
4. Cartesian Products

#### Linear Algebra :

##### 1. Definition of Vector Spaces

##### 2. Linear Independence, Spanning Sets, Bases, and Dimension

##### 3. Linear Maps, Isomorphisms, and Commutative Diagrams

##### 4. Coordinate and Matrix Representations

##### 5. Dual Spaces

##### 6. Multilinear Algebra: Tensors, Determinants, and Traces

##### 7. Norms and Inner Products

##### 8. Operators on Real Inner Product Spaces

###### Real Analysis :

###### 1. Definition of Metric Spaces

###### 2. Sequences in Metric Spaces and Finite-dimensional Real Normed and Inner Product Spaces

###### 3. Series in ℝ

###### 4. Topology of Metric Spaces and Finite-dimensional Real Normed and Inner Product Spaces

###### 5. Functional Continuity and Limits

###### 6. Differentiation in ℝ

###### 7. Differentiation on Finite-dimensional Real Inner Product Spaces (time permitting)

###### 8. Integration and Real Banach Spaces

###### Course Learning Outcomes

By the end of the course students should be able to:
- Prove sophisticated mathematical statements that only depend upon basic set and number theory, linear algebra, and real analysis.
- Rapidly and readily read and understand textbooks and proofs that only depend upon basic set and number theory, linear algebra, and real analysis.
- Enroll, and given suitable effort, perform acceptably in advanced engineering courses requiring a deep mathematical background.
