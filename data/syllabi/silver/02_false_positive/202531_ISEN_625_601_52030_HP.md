### Course Description

Fundamental methodologies of simulation modeling; random number and variate generation, statistical analysis of model output, and discrete event modeling using a commercial simulation language.





### Course Prerequisites

STAT 212 or STAT 601.



### Course Learning Outcomes

- Understand the fundamental methodologies of discrete event (process-oriented) simulation modeling.

- Understand the key statistical issues involved in simulation data preparation and the analysis of simulation output.

- Become familiar with modeling using a commercial language (Simio).

### Statement on the Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act (FERPA)

Currently enrolled students wishing to withhold any or all directory information items may do so by going to howdy.tamu.edu (https://howdy.tamu.edu/) and clicking on the "Directory Hold Information" link in the Student Records channel on the MyRecord tab.
