### Course Description

Selected topics in probability and data analysis for quality in engineering problems; measurement principles, data collection and data analysis to solve quality engineering problems; introduction to courses in the assurance sciences-reliability, maintainability, quality control and robust design.

### Course Prerequisites

None.

### Course Learning Outcomes

This course provides an introductory, hands-on treatment of broad aspects of the data analytics for engineers, with focus on statistical learning and predictive modeling methods. The course is designed mainly for master students pursuing professional degrees. At the end of this course, students should be able to

- (1) Learn the basic techniques of statistical learning and predictive modeling;

- (2) Learn the principles of building and validating modern statistical models for engineering applications;

- (3) Demonstrate your knowledge by implementing existing techniques, developing new techniques to solve real-world problems, and conducting a comprehensive and insightful review study on any emerging area of statistical learning and data mining.
